# Unknown-discovery domain profile

Historical actor/provider, host-path, and consumer-catalog details remain recoverable from pre-refactor blob `77611ac000dc66250d7c0346c12b9c1e9f463346`.

## Trigger
Load when a concrete discovery actor/provider, host carrier, consumer capability catalog, repository path convention, or downstream executor must be bound.

## Non-trigger
Do not load for generic known/unknown classification, probe generation, information-gain ranking, discovery budgeting, or handoff.

## Assumptions
Unknowns can be classified by knowledge/evidence state and probed through bounded actions whose expected information gain and cost are explicit.

## Specialization inventory
Named actors/providers, host prompt paths, consumer skill catalogs, and repository-specific discovery routes belong here.

## Evidence ceiling
A discovery hypothesis or provider response remains candidate evidence until verified by the evidence class required for the unknown.

## Fallback
If a preferred discovery capability is absent, emit the ranked unknown/probe packet for another executor and preserve the unavailable lane explicitly.

## Forbidden overrides
This module may not override `CORE-LAW-001` through `CORE-LAW-005`, relabel an unknown as known without evidence, hide failed probes, or widen provider/secret/merge authority.

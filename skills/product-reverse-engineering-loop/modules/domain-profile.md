# Product reverse-engineering domain profile

The portable body decides what a signal means and what a claim may close. This
module decides where signals come from and who owns the lanes the body cannot
enter. Nothing here is loaded unless its trigger matches.

## Trigger

Load when a concrete capture surface, product instance, retrieval tool, provider,
scheduler, forge, repository or Human admission authority must be bound to a run:
choosing how signals are captured, which product is the subject, which consumer
owns the user and paid lanes, or where a compiled handoff is executed.

## Non-trigger

Do not load for grading signals, classifying mechanisms, building the capability
graph, deriving closure states, compiling packets, or validating a prompt packet.
Those are core transitions and adding a concrete provider to them narrows a
portable law into one environment's habits.

## Assumptions

The subject is a product surface that can be observed repeatedly by somebody, and
the observation can be written down as a typed signal with a refutation
condition. When that is untrue — the surface is unreachable, the terms forbid
retrieval, or the behavior is not repeatable — the honest output is a signal set
with fewer signals, not a signal set with softer ones.

## Selection inventory

| Frozen need | Bind | Produced state |
|---|---|---|
| a concrete product surface and capture method | consumer capture adapter | `VERIFIED_BASE_BUILT` |
| user or paid evidence for a `USER`/`PAID` lane row | consumer evidence owner | closure row moves out of `NOT_EXERCISED` |
| execution of a compiled implementation packet | consumer runtime/worker adapter | `EXECUTABLE_HANDOFF` consumed |
| usage, licensing or commercial rights | Human admission record | `RGT-*` leaves `HUMAN_ADMIT_REQUIRED` |
| delivery of packets onto branches, issues or reviews | consumer delivery adapter | consumer-owned receipts |

Each row names a role, not a product. A consumer adapter binds the concrete tool
in its own repository and passes only the typed artifact back; a tool name in
this table would make the shared body describe one environment.

## Evidence ceiling

A capture run, a parsed page, a screenshot, a latency measurement or a tool exit
code is transport evidence about a surface. It is never evidence that a mechanism
is what the vendor says, that the workflow is the one users follow, or that
anybody would pay. A consumer receipt closes exactly the lane it was produced in
and no other. Fixture and synthetic evidence never become live evidence, and a
lane this repository cannot enter stays `NOT_EXERCISED` rather than being
downgraded to something that looks decided.

## Fallback

When a capture surface is unreachable or its terms forbid retrieval, record the
gap as an `ABSENT` slot with its owner and continue: a dossier that is honestly
thin is usable, and one padded with inference is not. When a user or paid lane
has no consumer owner, the closure row stays open with its owner named as the
consumer, and the loop still terminates at `EXECUTABLE_HANDOFF` with that row in
`remaining`. When a required right is unadmitted, stop — there is no fallback for
performing work the rights do not allow.

## Forbidden overrides

This module and any module it links may not override `CORE-LAW-001` through
`CORE-LAW-008`. It may not raise a signal's grade, reclassify an unobservable
mechanism as observable, close a row with an oracle from another lane, invent a
capability edge, merge two packets' leases, admit a usage right, promote fixture
evidence to live evidence, widen filesystem, network, secret or merge authority,
or activate itself because a capture tool happens to be installed.

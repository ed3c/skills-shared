# Research-to-MVP domain profile

Historical provider, consumer topology, artifact path, and prototype-host specifics remain recoverable from pre-refactor blob `f2818bf28e6ab7bc0e056668141195cfadd0bfc0`.

## Trigger
Load when a concrete research provider, prototype host, consumer loop, artifact location, market profile, or delivery environment must be selected.

## Non-trigger
Do not load for generic verified-base construction, gap closure, MVP slice selection, experiment design, graduation, or handoff.

## Assumptions
The input may be a research packet, specification, or partially verified concept whose claims and unknowns can be separated before implementation.

## Specialization inventory
Named model/provider roles, consumer loop layouts, prototype hosts, artifact directories, market-specific conventions, and downstream deployment routes belong here.

## Evidence ceiling
A working prototype or provider-generated plan does not establish product viability or correctness outside the tested MVP subject and acceptance contract.

## Fallback
If specialized providers/hosts are absent, emit a portable MVP packet with verified base, unresolved gaps, slice boundaries, assertions, and explicit unavailable lanes.

## Forbidden overrides
This module may not override `CORE-LAW-001` through `CORE-LAW-005`, promote unverified research into requirements, skip falsifying controls, or widen provider/secret/merge authority.

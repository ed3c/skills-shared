# Domain profile

Concrete provider, forge, session, workspace, review and observability integrations live here. The portable core may route to this module but may not import these names as canonical procedure.

## Trigger

Load this module only when the current task must bind a concrete provider, forge, session carrier, workspace tool, review service, observability implementation, or consumer command.

## Non-trigger

Do not load it for Human design admission, design-adversary separation, exact-subject binding, deterministic evidence ceilings, living-context routing, or delivery-authority laws that are already defined by the portable core.

## Assumptions

Provider implementations are replaceable adapters. Consumer repositories own credentials, paths, issue/PR identifiers, runtime sessions and live receipts. Rights/licensing review remains separate from deterministic method admission.

## Evidence ceiling

Provider availability, model agreement, review prose, dashboard output or a successful adapter call is candidate evidence only. None of these surfaces creates Human Admit, deterministic correctness, independent live Shadow, merge, release or production authority.

## Fallback

If no concrete adapter is required, remain on the portable procedure. If a required adapter is unavailable or its rights/authority boundary is unresolved, STOP that transition rather than silently substituting another provider.

## Forbidden overrides

This module may not override CORE-LAW-001 through CORE-LAW-005, weaken exact-subject binding, suppress material dissent, widen filesystem/network/secret/merge authority, or promote provider evidence to a stronger lane.

## Example adapter classes

Intent ledger adapters may target issue trackers or task ledgers. Review adapters may target native or third-party review tools. Session-observability adapters may target tracing or session dashboards. Workspace adapters may target worktree/workspace managers. Session carriers may target terminals or remote session systems.

Specific provider names, licenses and consumer bindings belong in consumer policy or a rights-reviewed adapter inventory, not in the portable core.

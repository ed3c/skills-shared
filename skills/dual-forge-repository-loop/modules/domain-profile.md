# Dual-plane repository domain profile

Historical host/runtime-specific details remain recoverable from pre-refactor blob `bd3559e730c9a4f16c95d72b675d9c8cae4c9f7f`.

## Trigger
Load when concrete local/remote forge hosts, CI provider, desktop/runtime carrier, credential route, publication adapter, or consumer repository binding must be selected.

## Non-trigger
Do not load for generic two-plane subject binding, local-first implementation, ancestry reconciliation, remote-state observation, publication admission, or exact-subject receipt law.

## Assumptions
At least two repository/transport planes may observe or mutate related Git subjects, and their identities/evidence states must remain distinct.

## Specialization inventory
Specific forge hosts, CI services, desktop/model carriers, local service endpoints, credentials, consumer remotes, issue/PR identities, and publication commands belong here.

## Evidence ceiling
Success on one plane proves only that plane. Local verification, remote forge state, CI, publication, integration, and merge remain independent evidence lanes.

## Fallback
If one plane is unavailable, preserve the local verified subject and reconciliation packet; do not infer the missing plane from another plane's receipt.

## Forbidden overrides
This module may not override `CORE-LAW-001` through `CORE-LAW-005`, conflate plane evidence, auto-resolve semantic conflicts, change visibility/access, expose secrets, or create merge/release authority.

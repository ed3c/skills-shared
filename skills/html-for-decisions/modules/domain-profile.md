# Decision-projection domain profile

Historical renderer, interaction, browser, and consumer-specific details remain recoverable from pre-refactor blob `7a770e3584f6ad439297a28187f102defdeba582`.

## Trigger
Load when a concrete renderer, interaction model, browser test adapter, visualization schema, email/export profile, or consumer UI contract must be bound.

## Non-trigger
Do not load for generic decision-SSOT binding, projection planning, evidence linking, deterministic validation, package/readback, or Human decision handoff.

## Assumptions
The decision source of truth is separable from any one visual renderer or delivery channel.

## Specialization inventory
Specific graph/UI schemas, layout systems, interaction states, browser test matrices, export/email profiles, and visual style rules belong here.

## Evidence ceiling
A rendered page or screenshot proves projection behavior only; it cannot establish the truth of the underlying decision claims without their source evidence.

## Fallback
If the selected renderer/browser/export adapter is unavailable, preserve a machine-readable decision projection packet and mark that lane unavailable.

## Forbidden overrides
This module may not override `CORE-LAW-001` through `CORE-LAW-005`, alter source claims to fit a renderer, hide unsupported decisions, or widen browser/network/secret/merge authority.

Historical market/media/provider/consumer specifics remain recoverable from pre-refactor blob `4210310a7a956f9fac4145885a284a081bce1e95`.

## Trigger
Load when a concrete source type, research provider, proposal schema, license rubric, consumer loop, or downstream storage surface must be bound.

## Non-trigger
Do not load for generic source intake, knowledge extraction, gap classification, research dispatch, evidence harvest, convergence, or handoff.

## Assumptions
Source and runtime identities are explicit; optional research providers are not procedural authority.

## Specialization inventory
Media/source adapters, named model/provider roles, market/proposal scoring, license profiles, consumer loop layouts, and downstream storage conventions belong here.

## Evidence ceiling
A provider result or proposal score is advisory until the requested claims are independently grounded and the bound research objective is asserted.

## Fallback
When a provider or source adapter is absent, preserve the source/gap packet and continue with direct evidence or return an explicit unavailable state.

## Forbidden overrides
This module may not override `CORE-LAW-001` through `CORE-LAW-005`, weaken source attribution, hide unresolved gaps, or widen provider/secret/merge authority.
